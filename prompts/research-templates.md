# Research templates

Fill-in-the-blank prompts for research, review, and analysis.
Replace each `{{placeholder}}` with your own facts. The example inside the braces shows the kind of value that belongs there.

## Review

### Review a Contract

Clause-by-clause risk review against a standard playbook.

> Review the attached {{contract type, e.g., MSA, SaaS agreement, services contract}} clause by clause. For each material clause, flag whether the terms are favorable, market-standard, or off-market for {{our role, e.g., the customer}}, explain the risk, and suggest specific redline language. Pay particular attention to indemnification, limitation of liability, termination, IP ownership, confidentiality, and auto-renewal.

### NDA / Vendor Risk Check

Spot risky terms in an NDA or vendor agreement fast.

> Assess the attached {{NDA or vendor agreement}} for risk to {{our company}}. Identify one-sided or unusual terms (scope of confidentiality, term and survival, data handling, liability, indemnity, term, and termination), rate the overall risk as low, medium, or high, and list the specific changes to negotiate before signing.

### Extract Obligations & Deadlines

Pull key obligations, dates, and renewal triggers.

> Extract every obligation, deadline, and key date from the attached {{agreement}}. Return a structured list covering effective date, term, renewal and notice windows, payment terms, deliverables, reporting duties, and any milestones, noting which party is responsible for each and citing the relevant section.

## Compliance

### Compliance Check (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP)

Check a document or process against a privacy regime.

> Assess {{the attached document or this process}} for compliance with {{applicable regime, e.g., GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act 2023}}. Identify the obligations that apply, where the current terms or process fall short, and the concrete steps needed to close each gap (data processing terms, consent, retention, subprocessor flow-down, breach notice, and data subject rights).

## Research

### Statutory Analysis

Interpret a provision and how it applies to your business.

> Interpret {{section/article number}} of the {{statute name, e.g., Securities Act of 1933, GDPR Article 6, Indian Contract Act 1872}} considering: (1) plain meaning and legislative intent, (2) how courts and regulators have interpreted it, (3) amendments and current applicability, and (4) the practical implications for {{our business or transaction}}.

### Compare Jurisdictions

Compare how different jurisdictions handle a legal issue.

> Compare how {{jurisdiction A, e.g., US}} and {{jurisdiction B, e.g., UK}} approach {{legal issue or doctrine, e.g., force majeure, data privacy, limitation of liability caps}}. Highlight key differences in statutory framework, regulatory interpretation, enforcement practice, and practical implications for {{context, e.g., cross-border contracts}}.

### Regulatory Landscape

Map regulations, agencies, and compliance requirements.

> Map the regulatory framework governing {{activity or industry, e.g., fintech lending, pharmaceutical advertising, data processing}} in {{jurisdiction}}. Identify the primary statutes, relevant regulatory bodies, key compliance requirements, licensing obligations, and recent enforcement trends or proposed changes.

### Market-Standard Terms

Find what terms are typical for a clause or deal type.

> What are the market-standard positions for {{clause or term, e.g., limitation of liability caps, indemnification scope, payment terms, MFN clauses}} in {{deal type, e.g., enterprise SaaS, commercial supply, M&A}} for {{our role}}? Explain the typical range, what is considered aggressive on either side, and which positions are worth pushing for.
