## Cancellation of removal under INA § 240A - Wilkinson v. Garland

Nonpermanent residents may obtain cancellation of removal under INA § 240A(b)(1) by showing 10 years' physical presence, good moral character, no disqualifying convictions, and 'exceptional and extremely unusual hardship' to a qualifying U.S. citizen or LPR relative. Wilkinson v. Garland (2024) makes the BIA's hardship determination judicially reviewable.
