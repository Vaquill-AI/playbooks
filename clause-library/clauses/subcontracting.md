## Subcontracting (balanced, consent, flow-down, prime responsibility)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Provider may engage subcontractors to perform portions of the Service, provided that Provider (a) remains fully responsible and liable for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors and for their compliance with this Agreement to the same extent as if performed by Provider; (b) imposes on each subcontractor written obligations consistent with this Agreement, including confidentiality, data-protection, and intellectual-property terms; and (c) does not disclose Customer's Confidential Information to a subcontractor except on a need-to-know basis. Provider will, on Customer's reasonable request, identify its subcontractors that have access to Customer Data, and Customer may object on reasonable, documented grounds relating to data protection or security.
