## Intellectual Property Ownership (deliverables to Customer, vendor retains tools)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Background IP. Each party owns and retains all right, title, and interest in and to all Intellectual Property Rights and materials that it owned or developed prior to or independently of this Agreement, together with any modifications, enhancements, and derivatives thereof ("Background IP"). Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of either party's Background IP to the other, except for the limited licenses expressly granted herein.

(b) Deliverables. Subject to Customer's payment of the fees due for the applicable Deliverables and excluding Vendor Tools (as defined below), Vendor hereby irrevocably assigns to Customer all right, title, and interest, including all Intellectual Property Rights, in and to the deliverables, work product, and materials that Vendor is engaged to create specifically for Customer under this Agreement and that are delivered to Customer (collectively, the "Deliverables"). To the extent any Deliverable qualifies as a "work made for hire" within the meaning of 17 U.S.C. Section 101, it shall be deemed a work made for hire and owned by Customer; to the extent any Deliverable does not so qualify, the foregoing assignment shall apply. Vendor shall, at Customer's reasonable request and expense, execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to perfect and record Customer's ownership.

(c) Vendor Tools and License-Back. Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in and to its Background IP and in any tools, methods, templates, libraries, know-how, and general-purpose components that Vendor uses to create the Deliverables or that are of general application (collectively, "Vendor Tools"). To the extent any Vendor Tools are incorporated into a Deliverable, Vendor grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, and create derivative works of such Vendor Tools solely as embedded in and necessary for Customer's use of the Deliverables.

(d) Feedback. If Customer provides Vendor with suggestions, ideas, or other feedback regarding Vendor's products or services ("Feedback"), Vendor may use the Feedback without restriction or obligation, and Customer grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use and incorporate the Feedback into Vendor's offerings, provided that Vendor does not identify Customer as the source.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- 17 U.S.C. 101 (Copyright Act, work made for hire)
- 17 U.S.C. 201 (Copyright Act, ownership)
- DTSA (18 U.S.C. 1836)

## Assignment of Inventions and Work Made for Hire (balanced, employee/contractor)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Work Made for Hire. All deliverables, works of authorship, inventions, and other materials conceived or developed by the Personnel in the course of performing under this Agreement (the "Work Product") are "works made for hire" under the U.S. Copyright Act to the extent permitted by law, and are owned by Company.

(b) Present Assignment. To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, the Personnel hereby irrevocably assign to Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all patent, copyright, trade-secret, and other intellectual property rights, effective automatically upon creation.

(c) Moral Rights; Further Assurances. The Personnel waive any moral rights in the Work Product and will, at Company's expense, execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to perfect and enforce Company's rights, and appoint Company as attorney-in-fact to do so if the Personnel are unavailable.

(d) Pre-Existing and Excluded IP. This assignment does not apply to inventions developed entirely on the Personnel's own time without Company resources that do not relate to Company's business, to the extent such an exclusion is required by applicable state law.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- 17 U.S.C. § 101 (work made for hire)
- Cal. Lab. Code § 2870

## IP Ownership with Pre-Existing IP Retained and License to Deliverables (permissive, provider-favorable)

**Tone:** permissive

### Clause text

(a) Pre-Existing and Background IP. Each party retains all right, title, and interest in its intellectual property existing before, or developed independently of, this Agreement, including Provider's tools, templates, methodologies, know-how, and software ("Provider Materials").

(b) Deliverables License. Provider retains ownership of the deliverables and the Provider Materials embedded in them, and grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, fully-paid license to use, copy, and modify the deliverables for Customer's internal business purposes.

(c) Customer Materials. Customer retains ownership of materials and data it provides and grants Provider a license to use them solely to perform the Service.

(d) Residuals. Provider may use the general knowledge, skills, and experience, and any ideas, concepts, and techniques retained in the unaided memory of its personnel, acquired during performance, provided it does not use Customer's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.
