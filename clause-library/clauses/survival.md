## Survival of Obligations on Expiration or Termination (mutual)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Survival. The expiration or termination of this Agreement, for any reason, shall not relieve either party of any obligation or liability that accrued before the effective date of such expiration or termination, nor of any obligation or liability arising out of any breach occurring before that date, and shall not affect any right or remedy that has accrued to either party as of that date. Any provision of this Agreement that, by its nature or express terms, is intended to survive expiration or termination shall so survive and remain in full force and effect, including the following (together with any related definitions and any Exhibits, Schedules, or Order Forms incorporated by reference to the extent they pertain to such provisions): (a) Confidentiality, for the period stated in that Section, and protection of trade secrets for so long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law; (b) ownership, assignment, and license of intellectual property and pre-existing materials; (c) representations and warranties, to the extent of their stated survival period; (d) indemnification; (e) limitation of liability and disclaimer of warranties; (f) accrued payment and reimbursement obligations, including any amounts that became due or were earned before the effective date of termination, together with applicable taxes and late charges; (g) audit and record-retention rights, to the extent expressly stated to survive; (h) return or destruction of materials and any post-termination transition or wind-down obligations; (i) governing law, dispute resolution, and venue or forum selection; and (j) the provisions of this Section and any provision addressing notices, no waiver, severability, and construction reasonably necessary to interpret or enforce the surviving terms. Where a Section specifies its own survival period, that stated period controls for that Section. The enumeration in this Section is illustrative and not exhaustive, and the omission of any provision from this list shall not be construed to mean that such provision does not survive if its survival is otherwise required by its nature or by law.
