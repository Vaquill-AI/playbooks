## Non-Compete (narrow, senior employee, 12-month, defined scope/territory, with consideration)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Covenant Not to Compete. In exchange for the Employee's employment or continued employment, access to Confidential Information, specialized training, and the additional consideration described in Section [__] (which the Employee acknowledges is good, valuable, and sufficient consideration the Employee would not otherwise receive), the Employee agrees that, during employment and for twelve (12) months after the termination of employment for any reason (the "Restricted Period"), the Employee will not, directly or indirectly, whether as an owner, officer, director, employee, consultant, or agent, perform services that are the same as or substantially similar to the services the Employee actually performed for the Company during the last twelve (12) months of employment for any business that competes with the specific products or services of the Company with which the Employee was materially involved or about which the Employee received Confidential Information (the "Competing Business"). This restriction applies only within the geographic territory in which the Employee actually worked or had material responsibility during that same period, or, if no such territory can be identified, the counties or states in which the Company actively conducted such business and in which the Employee's activities could cause competitive harm (the "Restricted Territory"). Nothing in this Section restricts the Employee from owning two percent (2%) or less of the publicly traded securities of any company, or from accepting employment with a non-competing division or affiliate of a Competing Business in a role that does not involve the products, services, or Confidential Information described above. The Employee acknowledges that these limitations are reasonable, are no broader than necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests in its Confidential Information, trade secrets, and customer goodwill, and do not unreasonably restrict the Employee's ability to earn a living. Drafting note: This covenant is governed by, and enforceable only to the extent permitted by, the law of the state whose law governs this agreement and the state in which the Employee primarily works. Post-employment non-competes are VOID and unenforceable for employees who primarily work in California (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 16600), Minnesota (Minn. Stat. 181.988), North Dakota, Oklahoma, Montana, and Wyoming, and several states (e.g., Washington, Colorado, Illinois, Oregon, Virginia, Maine) enforce non-competes only above statutory wage or salary thresholds and with advance-notice and consideration timing rules. There is no enforceable federal non-compete ban: the FTC 2024 Non-Compete Rule was vacated in Ryan, LLC v. FTC and abandoned by the FTC, so state reasonableness law controls. Use this covenant only for senior, well-compensated employees with access to trade secrets, and confirm wage thresholds, advance-notice, and consideration timing for the governing state. Where a non-compete is void or doubtful, rely instead on the confidentiality, trade-secret (DTSA, 18 U.S.C. 1836), customer/employee non-solicitation, and (where lawful) garden-leave provisions, which are independently enforceable and severable from this covenant.

- DTSA (18 U.S.C. 1836)

## Non-Compete (balanced, narrow, state-law savings; void where prohibited)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Covenant. During employment and for twelve (12) months after the Personnel's separation, the Personnel will not, within the defined territory, provide services to a competing business that are the same as or substantially similar to those the Personnel provided to the Company, where doing so would foreseeably involve the use or disclosure of the Company's Confidential Information or trade secrets.

(b) State-Law Limitations. This Section does not apply, and is void, to the extent prohibited by the law of the jurisdiction governing the Personnel's employment. Post-employment non-competes are unenforceable for most employees in California, Minnesota, North Dakota, and Oklahoma, and several states (for example, Washington, Colorado, Illinois, and Oregon) enforce them only above a statutory compensation threshold; in those jurisdictions this Section is of no force as to a covered employee.

(c) Reformation. If a court finds the duration, geographic scope, or activity restriction unreasonable, the parties intend the court to reform this Section to the maximum restriction that is enforceable rather than void it.

(d) Preserved Protections. Regardless of the enforceability of this Section, the Personnel's confidentiality, trade-secret, and non-solicitation obligations remain in full effect.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 16600
- Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1836)
