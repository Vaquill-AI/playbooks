## Data Protection (mutual, US state privacy + processor obligations)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Roles and Scope. To the extent Provider Processes Personal Information on behalf of Customer in connection with this Agreement, Provider acts as Customer's service provider, processor, or contractor (as those terms are used under applicable Data Protection Laws), and Customer acts as the business or controller. "Personal Information" means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that Customer provides to or makes accessible to Provider, and "Data Protection Laws" means privacy and data-security laws applicable to such Processing, including the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.100 et seq.), and other comprehensive U.S. state privacy laws to the extent applicable. (b) Use Limitation. Provider shall Process Personal Information only on Customer's documented instructions and solely to provide the services, and shall not sell or share Personal Information, retain, use, or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the services or as otherwise permitted by Data Protection Laws, or combine it with other data except as so permitted. Provider certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions. (c) Security Safeguards. Provider shall maintain reasonable and appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, including encryption of Personal Information in transit and at rest, access controls, and personnel confidentiality obligations, and shall make available, on request, a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO/IEC 27001 certification evidencing such controls. (d) Security Incidents. Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any confirmed unauthorized acquisition of or access to Personal Information, and shall reasonably cooperate with Customer's investigation and required notifications. (e) Subprocessors. Provider may engage subprocessors to Process Personal Information provided that Provider gives Customer prior notice of any new subprocessor (with a reasonable opportunity to object on reasonable data-protection grounds), imposes data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this Section by written contract, and remains liable for each subprocessor's performance. (f) International Transfers. Where Processing involves transfer of personal data originating in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy determination, the parties shall implement an approved transfer mechanism, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914) and, for UK data, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, which are incorporated by reference and prevail over conflicting terms to the extent of any conflict. (g) Assistance and Deletion. Provider shall reasonably assist Customer in responding to verified individual rights requests and in meeting Customer's obligations under Data Protection Laws, and upon expiration or termination shall, at Customer's election, return or delete Personal Information in its possession, subject to retention required by law.

### Applicable acts and authorities

## Data Protection (CCPA/CPRA service-provider terms, protective)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Roles. To the extent Provider processes Personal Information (as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA) on Customer's behalf, Provider acts as a "service provider" and Customer is the "business."

(b) Service-Provider Restrictions. Provider will not (i) sell or share Personal Information; (ii) retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than the specific purpose of performing the Service, or otherwise outside the direct business relationship with Customer; or (iii) combine Personal Information with information received from other sources, except as permitted by the CPRA. Provider certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions.

(c) Assistance. Provider will reasonably assist Customer in responding to verifiable consumer requests (access, deletion, correction, opt-out) and in fulfilling Customer's security and risk-assessment obligations.

(d) Subprocessors; Deletion. Provider will impose these obligations on any subprocessor by written contract, and on expiration or termination will delete or return Personal Information except as required by law.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- CCPA/CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.)

## Data Protection (GDPR Article 28 processor terms with SCCs, balanced)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Roles. Where Provider processes Personal Data subject to the EU/UK GDPR on Customer's behalf, Provider acts as processor and Customer as controller. Provider will process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to international transfers.

(b) Article 28 Obligations. Provider will (i) ensure persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality; (ii) implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures; (iii) engage subprocessors only under a written contract imposing equivalent obligations and with notice to Customer; (iv) assist Customer with data-subject requests and with its obligations under Articles 32 to 36; and (v) on termination, delete or return Personal Data and delete existing copies except as required by law.

(c) International Transfers. Where Provider transfers Personal Data outside the EEA or UK without an adequacy decision, the parties incorporate the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) by reference, which prevail in the event of conflict.

(d) Audits. Provider will make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits, which may be satisfied by current third-party certifications or reports.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- EU GDPR Article 28
- EU Standard Contractual Clauses
- UK GDPR / IDTA

## Information Security and Breach Notification (protective, SOC 2/encryption, notice without undue delay)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Security Program. Provider will maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards aligned to a recognized framework (for example, SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework), including encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, access controls on a least-privilege basis, secure software development practices, logging, and regular vulnerability testing.

(b) Breach Notification. Provider will notify Customer of any confirmed Security Incident affecting Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of confirmation, will provide the information reasonably necessary for Customer to meet its own notification obligations, and will not notify regulators or affected individuals on Customer's behalf without Customer's prior written consent except as required by law.

(c) Remediation. Provider will investigate, remediate, and mitigate the Security Incident at its expense, and will reasonably cooperate with Customer's investigation.

(d) Audits. On reasonable notice and no more than annually (or after a Security Incident), Provider will make available its current third-party audit reports and respond to reasonable security questionnaires.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- SOC 2
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
