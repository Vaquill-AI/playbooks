## Payment Terms (Net 30, dispute window, capped late charge)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Invoicing and Payment. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts set forth in each invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. All amounts are stated and payable in U.S. dollars by the payment methods designated by Vendor. Payment is deemed made when actually received by Vendor in immediately available funds. (b) Disputed Amounts. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts when due and shall, within fifteen (15) days after the invoice date, notify Vendor in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any such dispute promptly, and Customer's withholding of a properly disputed amount in accordance with this Section shall not be a breach of this Agreement. Any invoice not disputed within this period is deemed accepted as to the amounts stated. (c) Late Charges. Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall accrue a late charge of the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month (18% per annum) or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, calculated from the due date until paid in full. Exercise of this remedy is in addition to any other remedies available to Vendor. (d) Taxes. All fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, value-added, excise, and similar taxes, duties, and assessments imposed by any governmental authority on the amounts payable hereunder, excluding taxes based on Vendor's net income, property, or employees. If Vendor is legally obligated to collect or pay any such tax for which Customer is responsible, the tax will be invoiced to and paid by Customer, unless Customer timely provides a valid exemption certificate or other documentation acceptable to the relevant taxing authority.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- UCC Article 2 (Sales)
- State usury laws

## Payment Terms (protective, Net 45, late interest, suspension, no setoff)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Invoicing and Due Date. Provider will invoice Customer as set out in the applicable Order, and Customer will pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after the invoice date in U.S. dollars.

(b) Late Payment. Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid, and Customer will reimburse reasonable costs of collection.

(c) Suspension. If any undisputed amount is more than fifteen (15) days overdue, Provider may, on notice, suspend performance until paid, without limiting its other remedies.

(d) Taxes. Fees are exclusive of taxes; Customer is responsible for all sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes other than taxes on Provider's net income.

(e) No Setoff. Customer will pay all amounts without setoff, deduction, or withholding, except as required by law.
