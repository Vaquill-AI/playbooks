## Termination (mutual for cause, customer convenience, insolvency, effect + survival)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term stated in the applicable Order or Statement of Work, after which it will renew or expire as set forth there. (b) Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement, or the affected Order or Statement of Work, on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail; provided that for a failure to pay undisputed amounts when due, the cure period is ten (10) days after written notice. (c) Termination for Convenience. The Customer may terminate any Order or Statement of Work for convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Provider. If the Customer terminates for convenience, the Customer will pay for services performed and noncancelable commitments incurred through the effective date of termination, and the Provider will refund any prepaid fees for services not yet delivered as of that date. Fees for a fixed-term subscription are noncancelable and nonrefundable except as expressly stated in the applicable Order. (d) Insolvency. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files or has filed against it any petition under applicable bankruptcy or insolvency law that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or has a receiver appointed for substantially all of its assets. (e) Effect of Termination. On expiration or termination, all rights and licenses granted under this Agreement terminate, each party will promptly return or, at the disclosing party's written request, destroy the other party's Confidential Information in its possession, and all undisputed amounts accrued before the effective date of termination become immediately due. Termination does not relieve either party of any obligation that accrued before the effective date of termination and is without prejudice to any other remedy available at law or in equity. (f) Survival. Any provision that by its nature should survive termination, including provisions on accrued payment obligations, confidentiality, intellectual property ownership, warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, indemnification, and governing law, will survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## Termination for Convenience (balanced, either party, 30 days' notice, prepaid-fee refund)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) For Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement (or any affected Order) for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.

(b) Effect. Upon a termination by Customer for convenience, Customer will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination and is not entitled to a refund of prepaid fees. Upon a termination by Provider for convenience, Provider will refund to Customer any prepaid, unused fees for the period after the effective date of termination, calculated on a pro-rata basis.

(c) No Penalty. Termination for convenience under this Section is without liability or penalty, and is in addition to, not in lieu of, any other right or remedy.

## Termination for Cause with Cure Period and Suspension (protective, provider-favorable)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) For Cause. A party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach (ten (10) days for non-payment), or immediately if the breach is not capable of cure.

(b) Suspension. Without limiting its termination rights, Provider may suspend Customer's access to the Service upon notice if (i) Customer's account is overdue, (ii) Customer's use poses a security risk or may harm Provider's systems or other customers, or (iii) Customer is in breach of the acceptable-use terms. Provider will restore access promptly after the cause of suspension is resolved.

(c) Insolvency. Either party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to a bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

(d) Effect. Upon termination for Customer's breach, all unpaid fees for the remainder of the then-current term become immediately due.
