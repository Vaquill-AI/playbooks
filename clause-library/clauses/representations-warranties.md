## Representations & Warranties (mutual authority + service warranty + AS-IS disclaimer)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Mutual Representations. Each party represents and warrants to the other that: (i) it is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of formation; (ii) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and the person signing on its behalf is duly authorized to do so; (iii) this Agreement, when executed, will constitute its legal, valid, and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms; and (iv) its execution and performance of this Agreement will not violate, conflict with, or result in a breach of any law, judgment, or other agreement to which it is a party or by which it is bound.

(b) Service Warranty. Provider warrants that, for a period of ninety (90) days following acceptance of the applicable deliverable or completion of the applicable Services (the "Warranty Period"): (i) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards; (ii) the deliverables and Services will materially conform to the specifications and documentation set forth in the applicable Statement of Work or other writing signed by the parties; and (iii) to Provider's knowledge, the deliverables, as delivered and used in accordance with this Agreement, do not infringe or misappropriate any third party's patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret rights. As Customer's sole and exclusive remedy, and Provider's entire liability, for breach of the warranty in this Section (b), Provider will, at its option and expense, promptly re-perform or correct the nonconforming Services or deliverable, or, if Provider cannot do so within a commercially reasonable time after written notice given during the Warranty Period, refund the fees paid for the nonconforming Services or deliverable.

(c) Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS," AND PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED, STATUTORY, OR OTHERWISE, AND PROVIDER SPECIFICALLY DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, TITLE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT, AND ANY WARRANTIES ARISING FROM COURSE OF DEALING OR USAGE OF TRADE. To the extent the Uniform Commercial Code applies to any goods supplied under this Agreement, this disclaimer is intended to be conspicuous and to satisfy U.C.C. 2-316. Some jurisdictions do not allow the exclusion of certain implied warranties, so portions of this disclaimer may not apply where prohibited by law.
