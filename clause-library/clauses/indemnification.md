## Indemnification (mutual third-party claims, reciprocal procedure, subject to cap)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Each party (in such capacity, the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party and its affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents (collectively, the "Indemnified Party"), from and against any third-party claim, action, or proceeding (each, a "Claim"), and shall pay all damages, liabilities, settlements, and reasonable attorneys' fees and costs finally awarded against, or agreed to in settlement by, the Indemnified Party, to the extent the Claim arises out of (a) the Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement, (b) the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (c) bodily injury, death, or damage to tangible personal property caused by the Indemnifying Party's personnel. (a) The Indemnified Party shall (i) promptly notify the Indemnifying Party in writing of the Claim, provided that failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually and materially prejudiced thereby, (ii) grant the Indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the Claim, and (iii) provide reasonable cooperation at the Indemnifying Party's expense. (b) The Indemnifying Party shall not enter into any settlement that imposes any non-monetary obligation or liability on, or requires any admission of fault by, the Indemnified Party without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed. (c) The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense. Each party's indemnification obligations under this Section are subject to the limitation of liability set forth in this Agreement, except that the foregoing limitation shall not cap a party's obligation to pay amounts finally awarded by a court to a third party or agreed in a settlement of a Claim.

## IP Infringement Indemnity (provider-favorable, defense control, procure/replace/refund, carve-outs)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services or Deliverables, as furnished by Provider and used by Customer in accordance with this Agreement, infringe a United States patent, registered copyright, or registered trademark, or misappropriate a trade secret of such third party (an "Infringement Claim"), and shall pay damages and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded against Customer, or agreed to in a settlement approved by Provider, with respect to such Infringement Claim. As conditions to Provider's obligations, Customer shall (a) give Provider prompt written notice of the Infringement Claim, (b) grant Provider sole control over the defense and settlement of the Infringement Claim, and (c) provide all reasonable cooperation requested by Provider, at Provider's expense. If the Services or Deliverables are, or in Provider's reasonable opinion are likely to become, the subject of an Infringement Claim, Provider may, at its option and expense: (i) procure for Customer the right to continue using the affected item; (ii) modify or replace it with a functionally equivalent, non-infringing item; or (iii) if neither (i) nor (ii) is commercially reasonable, terminate the affected Services or license and refund to Customer the fees paid for the affected item, prorated over the applicable subscription or amortization period. Provider shall have no obligation under this Section to the extent an Infringement Claim arises from (a) combination of the Services or Deliverables with products, data, or services not supplied by Provider, (b) modification of the Services or Deliverables by anyone other than Provider, (c) Customer's specifications, designs, content, or instructions, (d) use after Provider has provided a non-infringing update or notice to discontinue, or (e) use in violation of this Agreement. THIS SECTION STATES PROVIDER'S SOLE AND EXCLUSIVE LIABILITY, AND CUSTOMER'S SOLE AND EXCLUSIVE REMEDY, FOR ANY INFRINGEMENT OR MISAPPROPRIATION CLAIM. Provider's obligations under this Section are subject to the limitation of liability in this Agreement.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- DTSA (18 U.S.C. 1836)
- Lanham Act (15 U.S.C. ch. 22)
- Patent Act (35 U.S.C.)
- Copyright Act (17 U.S.C.)

## Mutual Indemnification (permissive, narrow third-party claims, capped at fees)

**Tone:** permissive

### Clause text

(a) Mutual Indemnity. Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") will defend the other party and its officers, directors, and employees (the "Indemnified Party") against any third-party claim to the extent arising from the Indemnifying Party's (i) gross negligence or willful misconduct or (ii) breach of its confidentiality obligations, and will indemnify the Indemnified Party for damages and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded against, or agreed in settlement by, the Indemnifying Party.

(b) Cap. Each party's indemnification liability under this Section is subject to the limitations of liability in this Agreement, except for claims arising from a party's willful misconduct.

(c) Procedure. The Indemnified Party will promptly notify the Indemnifying Party of the claim, give it sole control of the defense (except that it may not settle a claim that imposes non-monetary obligations on the Indemnified Party without consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the Indemnifying Party's expense.

## Customer Indemnity (balanced, Customer data, content, and use of the Service)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Customer will defend Provider and its affiliates, officers, directors, and employees against any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Customer Data or any content, materials, or instructions Customer provides, including any claim that they infringe or misappropriate a third party's intellectual property or privacy rights; (b) Customer's use of the Service in violation of this Agreement, the documentation, or applicable law; or (c) Customer's products or services. Customer will indemnify Provider for damages and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded against, or agreed in settlement by, Provider. Provider will promptly notify Customer of the claim, grant Customer control of the defense (subject to Provider's right to participate with its own counsel at its expense), and provide reasonable cooperation at Customer's expense. Customer may not settle a claim in a manner that admits fault by Provider or imposes obligations on Provider without Provider's prior written consent.
