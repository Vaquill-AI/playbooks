# Limitation of Liability

## Limitation of Liability (mutual, 12-month fee cap, consequential waiver with carve-outs)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Exclusion of Indirect Damages. Except as provided in subsection (c), neither party will be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits, revenue, goodwill, anticipated savings, or business opportunity, or for the cost of substitute goods or services, in each case arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether based in contract, tort (including negligence), strict liability, or any other theory, and whether or not the party has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

(b) Aggregate Cap. Except as provided in subsection (c), each party's total cumulative liability for all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the total amounts paid or payable by Customer to Provider under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the most recent claim.

(c) Exclusions from Limitation. The exclusions and cap in subsections (a) and (b) do not apply to: (i) a party's indemnification obligations for third-party claims of intellectual property infringement; (ii) a party's breach of its confidentiality obligations; (iii) damages arising from a party's unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of, or failure to secure, the other party's data; (iv) Customer's payment obligations; or (v) liability arising from a party's fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct, or that cannot be limited or excluded under applicable law.

(d) Essential Basis. The parties acknowledge that the limitations in this Section reflect an agreed allocation of risk and form an essential basis of the bargain, and will apply notwithstanding the failure of essential purpose of any limited remedy.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- Uniform Commercial Code § 2-719
- Cal. Civ. Code § 1668

## Limitation of Liability (protective, fee cap with data-breach super-cap and uncapped carve-outs)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Exclusion of Indirect Damages. Except as provided in subsection (d), in no event will either party be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits, revenue, goodwill, anticipated savings, business interruption, or cost of cover or substitute services, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, regardless of the form of action and whether or not the party was advised of the possibility of such damages.

(b) General Cap. Except as provided in subsections (c) and (d), Provider's total aggregate liability for all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid by Customer to Provider under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the first event giving rise to liability.

(c) Data-Breach Super-Cap. For claims arising from a Security Incident, breach of Provider's data-protection or security obligations, or unauthorized access to, use of, or disclosure of Customer Personal Data, Provider's aggregate liability will not exceed three (3) times the amount of the general cap in subsection (b), inclusive of regulatory fines, notification costs, credit-monitoring, and forensic expenses, but exclusive of any amounts recoverable under subsection (d).

(d) Uncapped Matters. Nothing in this Section limits liability for: (i) Provider's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property infringement claims; (ii) breach of confidentiality obligations; (iii) a party's fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct; or (iv) any liability that may not lawfully be limited under applicable law, including, in California, liability that cannot be released under Cal. Civ. Code § 1668.

(e) Carve-Back; Essential Basis. The damages exclusion in subsection (a) does not apply to the matters described in subsections (c) and (d), and direct damages for those matters remain recoverable. These limitations allocate risk between the parties, form an essential basis of the bargain, and survive and apply notwithstanding the failure of essential purpose of any limited remedy.

- DTSA (18 U.S.C. § 1836)

## Limitation of Liability (permissive, customer-favorable, 24-month or USD floor cap)

**Tone:** permissive

### Clause text

(a) Cap. Except for the Excluded Matters in subsection (c), each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the greater of (i) the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, or (ii) USD 500,000.

(b) Indirect Damages. Except for the Excluded Matters, neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits or revenue, whether or not advised of the possibility.

(c) Excluded Matters (uncapped). The cap and the indirect-damages waiver do not apply to: (i) a party's indemnification obligations; (ii) breach of confidentiality or data-protection obligations; (iii) infringement or misappropriation of the other party's intellectual property; (iv) Customer's payment obligations; or (v) a party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.
