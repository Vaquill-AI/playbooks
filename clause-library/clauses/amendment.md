## Amendments and Order of Precedence (balanced, writing-signed, no oral modification)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Amendments. No amendment or modification of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by an authorized representative of each party. No oral statement, course of dealing, or course of performance modifies this Agreement.

(b) Order of Precedence. In the event of a conflict among the documents comprising this Agreement, the following order of precedence governs, unless an Order expressly states that it overrides a specific section by reference: (i) the body of this Agreement, (ii) any exhibits or addenda, and (iii) the applicable Order or statement of work.

(c) Click-Through Terms. Any conflicting or additional terms in a Customer purchase order, vendor portal, or click-through are void and of no effect.
