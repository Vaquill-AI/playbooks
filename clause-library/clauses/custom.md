## Artificial Intelligence and Generative-AI Use (balanced, output, IP indemnity, no training on Customer Data)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Permitted Use. To the extent Provider uses artificial intelligence or machine-learning models (including generative-AI systems) to provide the Service, Provider will do so in accordance with this Agreement, the documentation, and applicable law.

(b) Customer Data; No Training. Provider will not use Customer Data, Customer's inputs, or outputs generated for Customer to train, fine-tune, or improve any model made available to other customers or third parties, except using aggregated and de-identified data that cannot reasonably be associated with Customer.

(c) Output Ownership. As between the parties, Customer owns the outputs generated for it through its use of the Service, and Provider assigns to Customer any rights it may have in those outputs, subject to Provider's retained rights in the underlying models and Provider Materials.

(d) IP Indemnity. Provider's intellectual-property indemnification obligations extend to third-party claims that the Service's AI output, as delivered by Provider, infringes a third party's intellectual-property rights, excluding claims arising from Customer's inputs or Customer's modification or combination of the output.

(e) Human Oversight. Customer is responsible for reviewing AI-generated output before relying on it for any decision with legal or similarly significant effect.

## Anti-Corruption and Economic Sanctions (protective, FCPA, OFAC, export controls)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Anti-Corruption. Each party will comply with all applicable anti-corruption laws, including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act, and will not, directly or indirectly, offer, promise, give, or authorize any payment or thing of value to any government official or private party to obtain an improper advantage in connection with this Agreement.

(b) Sanctions and Export Controls. Each party will comply with applicable economic-sanctions and export-control laws, including those administered by the U.S. Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), the U.S. Export Administration Regulations, and the International Traffic in Arms Regulations. Neither party will provide the Service to, or use the Service in, a sanctioned jurisdiction or by a sanctioned or denied party.

(c) Representations. Each party represents that it and its owners, directors, and officers are not the target of sanctions and have not been convicted of, and are not under investigation for, a violation of anti-corruption or sanctions laws.

(d) Remedies. A breach of this Section is a material breach permitting immediate termination, and the breaching party will indemnify the other for resulting losses, fines, and penalties.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 78dd-1 et seq.)
- OFAC sanctions regulations (31 C.F.R.)
- Export Administration Regulations (15 C.F.R. Part 730 et seq.)

## Service Levels and Service Credits (balanced, uptime commitment, credits, chronic-failure termination)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Availability Commitment. Provider will make the Service available at least 99.9% of the time each calendar month, measured excluding scheduled maintenance (for which Provider gives at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice) and events outside Provider's reasonable control.

(b) Service Credits. If monthly availability falls below the commitment, Customer is entitled, as its primary remedy, to service credits on a tiered schedule (for example, 5% of the monthly fee for availability of 99.0% to 99.9%, scaling up for lower availability), claimed within thirty (30) days of the affected month.

(c) Chronic Failure. If the Service fails to meet the availability commitment in three (3) consecutive months, or in any four (4) months in a rolling twelve-month period, Customer may terminate the affected Order for cause without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid, unused fees.

(d) Support. Provider will provide support and incident response in accordance with the severity-based response and resolution targets in the applicable Order or support policy.

## Publicity and Use of Name (balanced, mutual prior written consent, limited customer-list license)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Neither party will issue any press release or public statement regarding this Agreement, or use the other party's name, logo, or trademarks in any advertising, marketing, or customer list, without the other party's prior written consent (email accepted), which may be withheld in its sole discretion. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Customer grants Provider a limited, revocable license to use Customer's name and logo solely to identify Customer as a customer on Provider's website and customer lists, subject to Customer's trademark usage guidelines and Customer's right to revoke that license on written notice. Any goodwill arising from use of a party's marks inures to the owner of the marks.
