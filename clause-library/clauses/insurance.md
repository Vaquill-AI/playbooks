## Insurance (mutual, market-standard limits with additional insured)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

Insurance. During the term of this Agreement and for any survival period expressly stated below, each party shall, at its own expense, maintain insurance with carriers having an A.M. Best rating of at least A- VII, in not less than the following amounts: (a) Commercial General Liability, including bodily injury, property damage, products and completed operations, and contractual liability, with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) where the party performs professional or technical services under this Agreement, Professional Liability (Errors and Omissions) of at least $2,000,000 per claim; (c) where the party will access, store, process, or transmit the other party's confidential or personal information, Cyber Liability (including privacy, network security, and data breach response) of at least $5,000,000 per claim; (d) Workers' Compensation at statutory limits and Employer's Liability of at least $1,000,000; and (e) if vehicles are used in connection with this Agreement, Commercial Automobile Liability of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit. Umbrella or excess coverage may be used to satisfy these limits. With respect to the Commercial General Liability, Automobile Liability, and Umbrella policies, each party shall name the other party as an additional insured, and such coverage shall be primary and non-contributory with any insurance maintained by the additional insured. Each party shall obtain a waiver of subrogation in favor of the other party to the extent of the indemnities given under this Agreement and permitted by the applicable policy. Upon request, and in any event before performance begins, each party shall deliver a certificate of insurance evidencing the foregoing coverages (and at minimum for the Professional Liability and Cyber Liability coverages), and shall provide at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction. These insurance requirements do not limit either party's liability or obligations under this Agreement.
