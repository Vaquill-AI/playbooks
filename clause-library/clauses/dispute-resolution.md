## Dispute Resolution (mutual escalation to AAA arbitration, FAA)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Informal resolution. Before commencing any formal proceeding, a party asserting a dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, breach, termination, or validity (a "Dispute"), shall give the other party written notice describing the Dispute and the relief sought. The parties' authorized representatives shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days after the notice. If the Dispute is not resolved, either party may submit it to non-binding mediation administered by the American Arbitration Association ("AAA") under its Commercial Mediation Procedures, with the parties sharing the mediator's fees equally.

(b) Binding arbitration. Any Dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days after a mediation request shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by the AAA under its Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect. The arbitration shall be seated in the home state of the responding party, and the arbitrator may award any relief available in a court of law or equity. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

(c) Arbitrator. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator, except that where the amount in controversy exceeds Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000), exclusive of interest and costs, it shall be conducted by a panel of three arbitrators.

(d) Governing law and scope. This clause evidences a transaction involving interstate commerce, and the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. ss. 1-16, governs its interpretation and enforcement. Each party bears its own fees and costs, and the parties share the AAA and arbitrator fees equally unless the arbitrator allocates them otherwise.

(e) Injunctive relief. Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking temporary or preliminary injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent actual or threatened breach of confidentiality or infringement or misappropriation of intellectual property, pending the appointment of the arbitrator.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- Federal Arbitration Act (9 U.S.C. 1-16)
- AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules
- AAA Commercial Mediation Procedures

## Dispute Resolution (protective, AAA arbitration + class-action waiver, EFAA carve-out)

**Tone:** protective

### Clause text

(a) Mandatory escalation. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, performance, breach, termination, or validity (a "Dispute"), through senior-executive negotiation within fifteen (15) days after written notice, followed, if unresolved, by mediation administered by the American Arbitration Association ("AAA") under its Commercial Mediation Procedures. Completion of these steps is a condition precedent to arbitration, except where a party seeks the equitable relief described in subsection (e) or where a limitations period would otherwise expire.

(b) Binding arbitration. Any Dispute not resolved through the foregoing steps shall be finally resolved by confidential, binding arbitration administered by the AAA under its Commercial Arbitration Rules then in effect, including the Procedures for Large, Complex Commercial Disputes where the amount in controversy exceeds Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000) (in which case three arbitrators shall be appointed; otherwise a single arbitrator shall preside). The arbitration shall be seated in, and the award rendered in, the home state of Provider, and judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction. This Agreement evidences a transaction in interstate commerce, and the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. ss. 1-16, governs the enforceability and interpretation of this clause.

(c) Class-action and consolidation waiver. To the fullest extent permitted by law, each party waives any right to bring or participate in any Dispute as a class, collective, consolidated, or representative action, and the arbitrator shall have no authority to arbitrate any Dispute on a class or representative basis or to consolidate the claims of more than one party. If this waiver is found unenforceable as to a particular claim, that claim alone shall proceed in court, and the remainder shall remain in arbitration.

(d) Statutory carve-out. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, this clause does not require arbitration of any claim that, at the electing party's option, may not be subject to a pre-dispute arbitration agreement under the Ending Forced Arbitration of Sexual Assault and Sexual Harassment Act, 9 U.S.C. ss. 401-402, or any other claim rendered non-arbitrable by applicable law.

(e) Equitable relief. Either party may seek temporary, preliminary, or permanent injunctive relief and specific performance from a court of competent jurisdiction, without posting bond and without waiving arbitration of any remaining claim, to address actual or threatened breach of its confidentiality, data-protection, or intellectual-property rights.

(f) Confidentiality and fees. The existence, content, and result of the arbitration shall remain confidential except as required by law or to enforce the award. The arbitrator shall award the prevailing party its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the extent permitted by applicable law.

### Applicable acts and authorities

- Federal Arbitration Act (9 U.S.C. 1-16)
- AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules
- AAA Procedures for Large, Complex Commercial Disputes
- EFAA (9 U.S.C. 401-402)
- AAA Commercial Mediation Procedures

## Dispute Resolution (balanced, escalation then litigation, no arbitration)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Escalation. Before commencing litigation (other than for injunctive relief), the parties will attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute by escalating it to senior executives with authority to settle, who will meet (in person or by video) within fifteen (15) business days after a party's written request.

(b) Litigation. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days after the escalation request, either party may pursue its remedies in the courts designated in the Governing Law section.

(c) Injunctive Relief. Either party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its intellectual property or Confidential Information without first complying with subsection (a).

(d) Fees. In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.
