## Force Majeure (mutual, suspends performance not payment, 60-day termination)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Definition. "Force Majeure Event" means an event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the party affected (the "Affected Party") that could not have been avoided or overcome through the exercise of reasonable diligence, including: acts of God, fire, flood, earthquake, hurricane, or other natural disaster; epidemic or pandemic; war, invasion, armed conflict, act of terrorism, riot, or civil unrest; embargo, blockade, or general failure of public utilities, telecommunications, or the internet; strike, lockout, or other labor disturbance not specific to the Affected Party's own workforce; and any binding order, law, or action of a governmental authority that prohibits or materially impedes performance.

(b) Effect. If a Force Majeure Event prevents, hinders, or delays a party's performance of its obligations under this Agreement, the affected obligations will be suspended for so long as, and to the extent that, the Force Majeure Event continues, and the time for performance will be extended accordingly. Neither party will be liable for any failure or delay in performance to that extent.

(c) Payment obligations not excused. A Force Majeure Event does not relieve either party of any obligation to pay amounts that are due or that become due for services already rendered or goods already delivered, except where the Force Majeure Event makes such payment legally impossible.

(d) Notice and mitigation. The Affected Party will give the other party prompt written notice of the Force Majeure Event, including a reasonable estimate of its expected duration, and will use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event and to resume full performance.

(e) Termination. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than sixty (60) consecutive days, either party may terminate this Agreement (or the affected Statement of Work or order) on written notice to the other, without liability except for obligations accrued before termination.

(f) Exclusions. A Force Majeure Event does not include any event that the Affected Party could reasonably have foreseen and guarded against, the unavailability or insufficiency of funds, general market or economic conditions, or any event arising from the Affected Party's own negligence or breach of this Agreement.
