## No Waiver (writing-signed; non-continuing; no course-of-dealing waiver)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

No waiver of any provision of this Agreement, or of any breach, default, right, or remedy under it, will be effective unless it is set forth in a writing that identifies the provision or right being waived and is signed by the Party against whom the waiver is asserted. No waiver granted under this Section will constitute a waiver of, or estoppel with respect to, any other or subsequent breach or default, whether of the same or a different character, and no such waiver will extend to or affect any provision other than the one expressly waived. A waiver on any one occasion will be limited to that specific occasion and instance and will not be construed as a continuing waiver or as a waiver of any future performance of the same obligation. No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right, power, or remedy under this Agreement will operate as a waiver of that right, power, or remedy, nor will any single or partial exercise of any right, power, or remedy preclude any other or further exercise of it or the exercise of any other right, power, or remedy. No waiver will be implied from, and neither Party may rely on, any course of dealing or course of performance between the Parties, any prior or subsequent indulgence or forbearance, any usage of trade, or the acceptance of any payment or performance, whether or not made or accepted with knowledge of a breach or of the existence of any right or remedy. The rights and remedies provided in this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not exclusive of, any rights or remedies available at law or in equity, and a Party's election to pursue one remedy will not preclude it from pursuing any other.
