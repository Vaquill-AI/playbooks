## Notices (mutual, certified mail / courier / email, effective on receipt or 3 business days)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

All notices, consents, demands, approvals, and other communications required or permitted under this Agreement (each, a "Notice") must be in writing and delivered to the receiving party at the address set forth below such party's signature, or to any other address that such party may designate by Notice given in accordance with this Section. A Notice may be delivered by any of the following methods: (a) personal delivery; (b) certified or registered United States mail, postage prepaid, return receipt requested; (c) a nationally recognized overnight courier with tracking and signature confirmation; or (d) email to the email address designated below, provided that the sender does not receive an automated bounce or non-delivery message and concurrently sends a copy by a method described in clause (a), (b), or (c). A Notice is deemed effective: (i) upon actual receipt if personally delivered or sent by email in accordance with clause (d); (ii) one business day after deposit with an overnight courier; or (iii) the earlier of confirmed receipt or three business days after deposit with the United States Postal Service. Notwithstanding the foregoing, any Notice alleging a breach of, electing to terminate, or asserting indemnification or a claim under this Agreement must be delivered by a method described in clause (a), (b), or (c) (and may not be given solely by email) in order to be effective. Either party may update its Notice address or designated recipient by giving Notice to the other party in accordance with this Section. References to a party's legal department or general counsel, where designated below, are for courtesy copies only and do not by themselves constitute effective Notice.
