## Audit Rights (balanced, annual, 30 days notice, SOC 2/ISO in lieu of on-site)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Audit. No more than once per calendar year, and upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Customer (or an independent third-party auditor designated by Customer and reasonably acceptable to Vendor, who is not a competitor of Vendor and who is bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement) may audit Vendor's records, systems, and facilities solely to the extent reasonably necessary to verify Vendor's compliance with this Agreement, including the fees invoiced and Vendor's security and data-handling obligations. Any such audit shall be conducted during Vendor's normal business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Vendor's operations, and in compliance with Vendor's reasonable safety, security, and access policies. (b) Reports in lieu of on-site audit. In lieu of an on-site audit of Vendor's security controls, Vendor may satisfy its obligations under this Section by providing, upon request, its most recent SOC 2 Type II report, ISO/IEC 27001 certification, or a comparable independent third-party audit report or attestation, and Customer agrees to rely on such report or certification where it reasonably addresses the matters in scope, except where applicable law or a documented regulatory request requires a more specific examination. (c) Confidentiality. All information disclosed or observed in connection with an audit is the Confidential Information of Vendor and may be used by Customer only to assess and enforce compliance under this Agreement. (d) Costs and findings. Each party shall bear its own costs in connection with an audit; provided that if an audit reveals (i) an underpayment exceeding five percent (5%) of the amounts properly due for the audited period, or (ii) a material non-compliance with Vendor's obligations under this Agreement, then Vendor shall reimburse Customer's reasonable out-of-pocket audit costs and shall promptly remediate the deficiency, and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days. This Section survives termination or expiration of this Agreement for one (1) year.
