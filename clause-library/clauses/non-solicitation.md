## Non-Solicitation (mutual, 12-month, employees & customers)

**Tone:** balanced

### Clause text

(a) Non-Solicitation of Personnel. During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following its expiration or termination (the "Restricted Period"), neither party will, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or engagement, or hire or engage, any employee or independent contractor of the other party with whom the soliciting party had material contact in connection with this Agreement. "Material contact" means involvement in the performance, supervision, or receipt of the services or deliverables under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the solicitation.

(b) Non-Solicitation of Customers. During the Restricted Period, neither party will knowingly and intentionally use the other party's Confidential Information to solicit, divert, or take away the business of any customer of the other party that the soliciting party identified or learned of solely through this Agreement, for the purpose of providing products or services competitive with those the non-soliciting party provides.

(c) Carve-Outs. Nothing in this Section restricts either party from: (i) conducting general advertising, public job postings, recruiting through agencies or job boards, or other solicitations not specifically directed at the other party's personnel or customers; (ii) hiring or engaging any individual who responds to such general solicitation, who approaches the party on an unsolicited basis, or who has not been employed or engaged by the other party for at least six (6) months; or (iii) soliciting or doing business with any customer that independently and without targeted inducement initiates contact or that the party had a pre-existing relationship with before the Effective Date.

(d) Remedies and Reasonableness. The parties agree these restrictions are reasonable and necessary to protect their respective legitimate business interests, including Confidential Information and customer goodwill. The parties acknowledge that the enforceability of restrictive covenants is governed by the law of the applicable state and that this Section is to be enforced only to the maximum extent permitted by such law; if any portion is held overbroad, it will be reformed or limited rather than void, to the extent the governing jurisdiction permits reformation. A breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and the non-breaching party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedy.
