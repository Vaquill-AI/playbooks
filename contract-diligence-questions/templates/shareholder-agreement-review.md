# Shareholder Agreement Review

15 columns: parties, share classes, board, reserved matters, pre-emption, drag/tag-along, exit, deadlock, restrictive covenants.

15 questions.

| # | Column | Question | Answer type | Options |
|---|--------|----------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Parties | Identify all parties (company, founders, investors, classes of shareholders). | Free text |  |
| 2 | Share classes | List all share classes and their principal rights (voting, economic, preference). | Free text |  |
| 3 | Capitalization snapshot | What is the agreed capitalization or cap-table position at signing? | Free text |  |
| 4 | Board composition | How is the board composed? Who appoints which seats? | Free text |  |
| 5 | Reserved matters / consent rights | What matters require investor or class consent? Quote thresholds. | Free text |  |
| 6 | Pre-emption rights | What pre-emption / anti-dilution rights apply on new issuances? | Free text |  |
| 7 | Transfer restrictions | What restrictions apply to transfers of shares (ROFR, ROFO, lock-up)? | Free text |  |
| 8 | Drag-along | Is there a drag-along right? Summarize trigger thresholds and carve-outs. | Free text |  |
| 9 | Tag-along | Is there a tag-along right? Summarize scope and exceptions. | Free text |  |
| 10 | Information rights | What information / inspection rights do investors hold? | Free text |  |
| 11 | Liquidation preference | What is the liquidation preference (multiple, participation, seniority)? | Free text |  |
| 12 | Exit / IPO obligations | What exit, IPO, or registration-rights obligations are agreed? | Free text |  |
| 13 | Deadlock resolution | How are board or shareholder deadlocks resolved? (Swing vote, buy-sell, mediation.) | Free text |  |
| 14 | Non-compete / non-solicit on founders | What non-compete or non-solicit obligations apply to founders? | Free text |  |
| 15 | Governing law and dispute resolution | What law governs and what is the dispute-resolution forum? | Free text |  |
